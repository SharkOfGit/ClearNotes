Focus Detox

Identify your distractions and reclaim your study time. High-performance students don't just study harder; they remove the noise.

Common Distractions

Short-Form Content

YT Shorts, Reels, and TikTok reduce your attention span. Set a time limit.

Leisure Eating

Snacking when bored mimics hunger. Drink water and reset your focus.

Escape Napping

Napping because a topic is "hard" is a choice, you can choose.

Excessive Gaming

Games provide instant dopamine. Studying provides long-term serotonin.
