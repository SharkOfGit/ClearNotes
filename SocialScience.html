What is the informal sector?

The informal sector is the part of the economy that includes businesses and workers operating outside of government regulation, registration, and oversight. It mainly consists of manual labour and high-effort tasks in the blue-collar area, but it accounts of 90% of all jobs contributing in the Indian economy.


History: the EIC

British colonizers initially landed in India on August 24th, 1608 as the EIC. They arrived under the guise of foreign trade, but over the years slowly took control of the Indian subcontinent. For centuries, the British ruled India, ruining the Indian economy, forcefully attacking, prosecuting and hanging protestors and legendary revolutionaries. On August 15th of 1947, India finally attained Independence.
